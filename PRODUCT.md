# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Website owners, SaaS founders, marketers, and developers looking to improve their site's user experience (UX) and conversion rates (CRO) without hiring expensive consultants.

## Product Purpose
UXAuditX visits a user's live website URLs, captures layout sections on desktop and mobile viewports, runs multimodal AI vision models to spot design and conversion flaws, and generates a prioritized, screenshot-backed PDF report with clear fixes in under a minute.

## Brand Personality
- **Voice/Tone:** Direct, expert, evidence-based, clean.
- **Three-Word Personality:** Professional, sharp, modern.
- **Emotional Goals:** Instill confidence, clarity of issues, and urgency to optimize.

## Anti-references
- Beige-and-cream SaaS templates ("AI slop" styles).
- Generic card grids repeating identical layouts with boring icons.
- Non-functional glassmorphic panels and hard-to-read low-contrast gray text on dark backgrounds.
- Numbers/Metrics templates without visual evidence.

## Design Principles
- **Evidence over numbers:** Every issue must highlight the exact location on the page.
- **Immediate actionability:** State issues in plain English with specific, implementable fixes.
- **Viewport symmetry:** Equal care given to desktop and mobile layouts since over half of traffic is mobile.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- Strict contrast checks for typography (minimum 4.5:1 for body copy).
- Support for keyboard navigation and screen readers.
- Motion reduction media query support for interactive elements.
